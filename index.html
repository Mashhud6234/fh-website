FAQ

Questions? Answers here

Anything else? Send us a message on WhatsApp.

How long does the scent last?

Our Eau de Parfum lasts around 6 to 8 hours on skin, and even longer on clothes.

Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?

We deliver all over Pakistan within 4 to 6 working days. The delivery charge depends on your city and is shown before you place your order.

How do I pay?

Cash on Delivery. You pay the rider in cash when your parcel arrives.

Are the perfumes unisex?

Yes. Elite and Shadow are bolder, Neon and Gem are brighter and softer. No rules though, wear what you love.

How should I apply it?

Spray on pulse points like your wrists, neck and behind the ears, from about 15 cm away. Don’t rub your wrists together, it breaks down the scent faster.

How should I store my perfume?

Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, with the cap closed. Avoid leaving it in a hot car or bathroom.

What about returns or exchanges?

If your bottle arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, message us on WhatsApp within 3 days with an unboxing video and we’ll replace it. See our Shipping & Returns policy.
