Shipping & Returns

Where we deliver: all over Pakistan.

Delivery time: 4 to 6 working days after your order is confirmed.

Delivery charges: depend on your city and are shown at checkout before you place your order.

Payment: Cash on Delivery. You pay the rider when your parcel arrives.

Order confirmation: we call or WhatsApp you on the number you give us to confirm your order before we send it.

Damaged or wrong item: message us on WhatsApp within 3 days of delivery with an unboxing video (recorded while opening the parcel) and clear photos. After we check it, we will replace the item at no extra cost.

Change of mind: because perfume is a personal-care product, we can’t take back opened or used bottles.

Refused parcels: if a Cash on Delivery parcel is refused at the door without a valid reason, we may ask for delivery charges in advance on future orders.

Privacy

Why we collect it: only to confirm and deliver your order, contact you about it, and (if you joined our list) send you news about new drops and offers.

Where it is stored: securely in our order database (Google Firebase). Only the FH owner account can read it.

Who we share it with: only the courier company, and only the details needed to deliver your parcel. We never sell your information.
